---
title: "First steps with Hugo"
date: 2026-09-29
---

I had never used Hugo before, so I started with the official Quick Start and
built the smallest possible site: a config file, a couple of Markdown files and
a layout.

What surprised me most is how fast it is. Running `hugo server` rebuilds the
site and refreshes the browser in milliseconds every time I save a file.

Key idea: content lives in Markdown files under `content/`, and layouts in
`layouts/` decide how that content becomes HTML.
